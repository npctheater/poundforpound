Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Pound for Pound is a virtual reality boxing game for Meta Quest made by NPC Theater ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who it's shared with, and how to ask us to delete it. We try to collect as little as we can, and none of it is sold.

The short version

  • You don't create an account with us. The game makes an anonymous ID for you on first launch.
  • We never collect your real name, email address, contacts, location, voice or camera images.
  • Some game data, such as how your head and controllers moved during a fight, can be sent to our servers to help us tune the opponent and the punch-damage system. It's tied to your anonymous ID, not your identity.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing support@poundforpoundvr.com.

Information the game collects

1. Your anonymous game account

On first launch the game creates a random identifier and uses it to sign in to Microsoft PlayFab, the service that stores game accounts and settings. PlayFab gives your account a player ID, and the game gives your boxer an auto-generated display name. This account isn't linked to your Meta account. PlayFab may also receive basic technical details about your headset (such as device model, operating system and memory) and, like any internet service, your IP address when the game connects.

2. Purchase check

When the game starts, it asks Meta whether your Meta account owns Pound for Pound. We only receive a yes or no. We don't receive your Meta username or user ID.

3. Gameplay and motion data

Boxing in VR means the game reads where your headset and controllers are. Most of that stays on your headset. Some of it may be uploaded:

  • Heavy-bag recordings, only when you press RECORD. The bag menu has a RECORD button. A recording is up to about 60 seconds and includes the position and rotation of your headset and controllers, where and when your punches hit the bag, your calibrated standing height and arm reach, the size of your cleared play area (its four corners, nothing about your room), your headset model, the game version and the reason you chose for recording.
  • Fight recordings in test builds. In pre-release test builds (for example our private Alpha), each fight against the computer opponent is recorded and uploaded automatically: your head and controller motion, the opponent's motion, the punches and game events, your standing height and arm reach, the fighter and difficulty you picked, and the game version. Public store builds don't record fights for upload unless we tell you otherwise in a future update of this policy.

We use this data to make the opponent react more naturally and to grade punches more fairly. Uploads are tied to your anonymous PlayFab player ID.

4. Passthrough (optional)

If you turn on passthrough in the settings, the game asks for permission to use the headset cameras. It samples a tiny, very low-resolution version of the camera image on your headset to match the game's lighting to your room. Camera images are never saved or sent anywhere.

5. Stored on your headset only

Your settings, calibration (height, reach, floor height), play-area preferences, recent fight replays and game progress are saved on your headset. This stays on the device unless one of the uploads above includes it. Uninstalling the game removes it.

What we don't collect

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, we don't record audio, and there are no in-app purchases. The game doesn't use hand tracking, face tracking, eye tracking or Meta body tracking.

Online play (coming later)

Online multiplayer isn't available yet. When it arrives, the game will need to use your Meta user ID and Horizon username so opponents can see who they're fighting, and your Meta block list so you're never matched with people you've blocked. A match will also send your head and controller movements to your opponent in real time so they can see your fighter move. We'll update this policy before that happens.

Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. It's processed only by the service providers that run the game for us:

  • Microsoft PlayFab: game accounts, settings and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: receives and stores uploaded recordings (Cloudflare Workers and R2 storage).
  • Meta: purchase check and, later, online features, under Meta's privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Uploaded recordings are kept for up to 3 years and then deleted, or sooner if you ask. Your PlayFab account is kept while you play and deleted when you ask us, or after 3 years without any sign-in.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Don't press RECORD on the heavy bag if you don't want a bag recording uploaded.
  • To have your data deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email support@poundforpoundvr.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include your boxer's display name (shown in the game menu) so we can find your account. We'll delete your PlayFab account and every recording linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as access, correction, deletion and objection. Contact us at the same address to use them.

Children

Pound for Pound is made for players aged 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we'll delete any associated data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and uploads are accepted only from a signed-in game session. Stored recordings are kept in private storage that only we can access.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the game collects, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches players.
