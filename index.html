Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have feedback? Email support@poundforpoundvr.com. It helps to include your headset model, the game version (shown in the game menu) and what happened just before the problem.

Quick fixes

  • Fighter feels too close or far: recenter your view with the Meta button, then re-run the height and reach calibration from the menu.
  • Not enough room: clear at least 2 x 2 m of floor. The game warns you near your boundary.
  • Want your data deleted? See the Privacy Policy.

Pound for Pound is made for players 13 and older. Play in a safe, cleared space and take breaks.
