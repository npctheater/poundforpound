01 Punch grading

How punches score

Damage depends on mechanics, commitment, and target. Clean shots to the chin, liver, or open body carry weight; loose slaps and wrist flicks register for very little.

Filtered out

  • Arm-only hooks
  • Wrist-flick punches
  • Stiff-arm hooks
  • Flail spam
  • Head-bob exploits
  • Jumping and running

Combinations and counters

Clean combinations build damage across an exchange, and catching an opponent mid-punch counts as a counter.

02 Single player

An opponent that boxes back

Animated from real boxers' motion, opponents slip, block, and roll with incoming punches. They track your patterns, punish an open guard, and adjust across rounds, from Easy to Expert.

03 In the ring

Full-body presence

Your headset and two Touch controllers drive a full-body boxer in the ring, matching your stance, weight shift, and rotation without extra trackers.

Support

Found a bug, stuck on setup, or have feedback? Email support@poundforpoundvr.com with your headset model, the game version shown in the menu, and what happened right before the issue.

Quick fixes

  • Fighter feels too close or far: recenter your view with the Meta button, then re-run height and reach calibration from the menu.
  • Not enough room: clear at least 2 × 2 m of floor space. The game warns you near your boundary.
  • Data deletion requests: see the Privacy Policy.

Pound for Pound is rated for players 13 and older. Play in a clear space and take breaks.
